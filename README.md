# Ahmed Hamza

Political scientist studying ethnic politics, political parties and democratization. Ph.D. candidate in Political Science at the University of North Texas.

Main website: [ahmedhamza.org](https://ahmedhamza.org)

## Research

My research asks how political parties come to represent ethnic groups, and what that means for elections, partisanship and democracy. My dissertation, *The Causes and Consequences of Party Ethnicization*, builds a new measure of how much a party's support is concentrated in one ethnic group, with a focus on African democracies. I also work on Kurdish politics and political violence.
